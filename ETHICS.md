This is a personal learning project for understanding how large language models
work, including how they can fail or be misused, so that I can recognize and
defend against those failures.

Educational purpose only. Nothing here is meant to attack a real system.
Local and sandboxed. Every experiment ran on my own machine or my own Google
Colab session, against small open models. No real service, website, or person
was targeted.

Attacks are kept defensive. Where an exercise demonstrates a weakness
(prompt injection, data poisoning), the repository keeps the measurement and
the defense, not a usable attack. Examples use harmless invented facts.

No sensitive material. No real credentials, no model weights, and nothing
that imitates a real brand or organization is included.
